# FEEDBACK 
## STARBASE by lanna and aliyah
STARBASE is a game focused on data from a star database (I don't remember which precisely). The user plays as a worker with a task of identifying what star the astronauts went to, however some data of the star is missing.

I was impressed by their project, because it is engaging but also visually pretty to look at. The scenario of being a worker and having to search in a database about stars is fun! The aesthetic is definitely suiting.

I'm not sure what my suggestion would be. I think just continuing on the project and elaborate the already given features are a good direction. Maybe a timer or scoreboard could be fun, giving it a competitive aspect.

## YOU.EXE by simon
YOU.EXE seems like a normal personality test, but it is actually commentary on them. I get the impression that he seems to find them superficial to a degree. The visuals are very scientific and formal looking. The percentage of the results are completely random, showing how we can blindly trust things, specifically those that have a more professional aesthetic.

I'm very fond of personality tests, so I was impressed someone did one. I didn't think of it, but it makes sense given that it is based on the answers (data) that the user inputs. It definitely inspires me to make my own kind of personality test. It also makes me wonder how exactly the code works.

My suggestion for the future would be to have more questions and more personality results. I think it could help selling it more, because this one was very quick, though I understand that it could have been the point of how such a short quiz cannot accurately guess someone's personality.

## myspace by nerly
Nerly's project is her take on the myspace platform. It allows users to fill out a bunch of questions, and then it displays the results to the questions. The questions range from age to pronouns and birthday, to things like favourite colours, music, etc. There is a wide variety of questions. It displays with the help of json. 

It's aesthetic would definitely be its strongest point. Rather than let users customize their own page like on myspace, this already offers a platform that isn't a blank state. 

I think my main suggestion would just to continue on elaborating what is already done. There is a good base there. Some features that could be added could be seeing past entries from other people, like a collection. Maybe alternate themes like "dark" or "light" modes that are often offered by sites could be added. There could also be more customizing on the layout, change of fonts or colours, etc.
